SospechApp Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-04

SospechApp is a local social game. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the app.

1. What data do we collect

  • Local game data: game settings and preferences stored on your device.
  • Advertising: the app displays ads using Google AdMob. Ad providers may process device identifiers and technical data to display and measure ads.
  • We do not request sensitive permissions such as camera, microphone, or location for the main functionality of the game.

2. How is the data used

  • To ensure the game works correctly on your device.
  • To display ads and, where applicable, manage consent and ad performance measurement.

3. Third-party providers

The app uses Google services for advertising and consent (e.g., AdMob and User Messaging Platform). See also Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Consent and options

In regions where required, a consent form will be shown to manage ad preferences. You can review your device's privacy settings to limit ad personalization.

5. Data retention

Local data remains on your device unless you delete it by clearing data or uninstalling the app. Data processed by ad providers is subject to their own retention policies.

6. Children

The app is not specifically designed for children. If you choose to target children on Google Play, you must adjust ad settings and compliance accordingly.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, you can contact: manuacedevops@gmail.com

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect legal or technical changes. The update date will be adjusted on this page.
